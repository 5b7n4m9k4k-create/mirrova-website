Features

Private by default. Easy by design.

Mirrova keeps the complicated parts out of your way. You just chat.

Personas

One you, many reflections. Keep work, family, dating and selling apart. Each persona is its own identity, designed to keep your personas separate, so each person sees only the side of you that you choose.

Contact Firewall

Strangers can't reach you unless you let them in. Like a doorman for your inbox.

People you invited Allowed
Unknown sender Waiting at the door

No number. No email.

Sign up without a phone number or an email address. Nothing to leak, nothing to look up.

End-to-end encrypted

Built on MLS (RFC 9420), the open messaging security standard from the IETF, using the OpenMLS library.

Private notifications

Designed so your lock screen can hide who wrote and what they said. Notification privacy options are being built for the beta.

Disposable personas Coming later

A persona for one deal or one date. When it's done, let it go.

How it works

Three steps. No paperwork.

  1. 1

    Create your Mirrova

    Open the app and pick a name. No phone number, no email, no forms.

  2. 2

    Share a side of you

    Show a QR code or send a link. Choose which persona they get.

  3. 3

    Chat privately

    Messages are end-to-end encrypted. Only the people in the chat can read them.
